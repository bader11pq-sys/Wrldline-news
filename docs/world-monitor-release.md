# World Monitor and event-country release

- The monitor remains a read-only consumer of `public_world_monitor_feed`, limited to published classified reports in the previous 24 hours and 1,000 results. The 1/6/24 hour controls use publication time, not time of ingestion. There are no provider, classifier, RPC or database migrations for this release.
- Severity comes from the classified event's enumerated level. The numeric score can break a tie within a tier, but neither changes its tier nor appears as a country score. Neutral means no matching signal, not safety. The frontend never substitutes a publisher country for a missing event location.
- Boundary data: [Natural Earth public-domain terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) and [v5.1.2 1:110m Admin 0 Countries](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/f1890d9f152c896d250a77557a5751a93d494776/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson). The vendored GeoJSON retains the original 177 geometries and only keeps `ADMIN` plus `ISO_A3_EH` (mapping `-99` to an empty code). It is a low-resolution reference map, not an authority on borders. Countries with a signal but no polygon remain selectable through the list. The reduced file's SHA-256 is `eb1a26fd98a1f83f592c5950449a19c0d54a35c331a364b0311b049107bbdfb1`.
- [Leaflet 1.9.4](https://leafletjs.com/download.html) is self-hosted with its license and integrity hashes; CSS and license line endings and trailing whitespace are normalized. The monitor does not contact a tile provider.
- The live article template is in the separate `wrldline-public` Cloudflare Worker, not GitHub Pages. The template matches the stored event-country field to an Arabic or English ISO name (including known aliases) and suppresses region, combined and unknown labels. Confirm the active Worker deployment/version, configuration and existing routes against `cloudflare/workers/wrldline-public/wrangler.jsonc` before release. Bundle/test the exact approved commit with Wrangler dry-run, use the verified production account and existing Worker, then verify an article with a recognized `event_country` and one without it, the archive and the public feed. Do not change or deploy `wrldline-jobs` or `news-global-api`. Revert a bad Worker release to its previously recorded deployment; the static monitor can be reverted independently through the repository.
