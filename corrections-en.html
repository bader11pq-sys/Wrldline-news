NEWSROOM ACCOUNTABILITY

Corrections and right of reply

If something we published is inaccurate, or you have a material response to a story that concerns you, tell us what needs review.

Have the story link?

Use the existing correction form. Do not attach private documents.

What to put in your report

  1. Include the story URL and identify the specific paragraph, image, or headline.
  2. Explain what you believe is incorrect and what wording or information you propose we review.
  3. Add a public evidence link if available. Do not send anyone else's personal details or unpublished documents.

We prioritize reports by the importance of the error and the potential harm. A report is not proof of an error and does not trigger an automatic edit. We do not promise a fixed response time.

A correction is not an update

Correction
We fix a material error and, where possible, add a dated note explaining what changed on the story.
Update
We add a later development or new information without changing the original publication date.
Withdrawal
An editor reviews whether to remove a story whose basis failed or whose material harm cannot be fixed, and records the reason.
Duplicate removal
Hiding a duplicate is not a factual correction. We retain a reference version subject to editorial review.

Request a right of reply

If a story concerns you or an organization you represent, use the same form and write “Request for reply” in the message. Identify the statement, briefly explain your role, and provide a concise reply with public evidence if possible.

The newsroom reviews relevance, evidence, and privacy. Replies are not published automatically. Do not use the public form for anything requiring a secure channel.

Story labels

“Breaking” is a display priority; analysis and opinion are not straight reporting. See the guide to verification labels for unverified, disputed, and confirmed claims.

Translation and time errors

For translation issues, name the edition and link to the original if it is public. Receipt time is not publication time. Material errors in names, numbers, dates, or meaning can be reported.
